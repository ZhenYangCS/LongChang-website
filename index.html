PROCESS ILLUSTRATION
Read the abstract +

Can large language models (LLMs) uncover the laws and limits of their world through autonomous exploration and experimentation? To evaluate this ability in a controlled setting, we introduce LongChang, an automated framework that creates simulated worlds with altered physics, where one or more laws differ from ordinary physics. LLMs explore freely without research tasks or hints about these laws, manipulating objects and building tools to investigate questions they choose. LLMs generate scenes, review tools, and evaluate reports for correctness and experimental support using the altered physical laws and exploration records. These records guide scene evolution for further exploration, allowing the full cycle to run without human intervention. Among 12 LLMs, GPT-6-Astra achieves a discovery rate of 16.7%, while the other 11 score 0%. The exploration records reveal uninvestigated anomalies and only partial identification of the altered physical laws.
